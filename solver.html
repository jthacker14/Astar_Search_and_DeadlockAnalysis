How to Solve the AS Deadlock (The A* Solution)

This shows a solution for a key deadlock in **Column 5**: the AS (Ace of Spades) is stuck under a 3S.

  1. Find a place to move the 7H (from Col 5).
  2. Find a place to move the 7D (from Col 5).
  3. Find a place to move the 10H (from Col 5).
  4. Find a place to move the 8C (from Col 5).
  5. Move the AC (from Col 5) to the ♣ foundation.
  6. Move the 3S (from Col 5) to a free cell or another column.
  7. **Move 7:** Now the AS is free! Move the AS (from Col 5) to the ♠ foundation.

The A* solver and the $m_e(n)$ heuristic are what find this specific 7-move (or longer) optimal path out of all possible moves!
